Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 1, 2026

In short:

You do not need an account to use this app. The calculations you enter, and anything you choose to save, stay on your phone. We do not receive them. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to do that.

1. About this policy

Chakriya Byaj ("we", "us") provides the Android app Chakriya Byaj (the "app"), a calculator that works out compound interest between two Nepali (BS) dates. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information the app handles on your device

  • Calculation details. The dates, principal amount and interest rate you type in, and the result. They are used only to show you the result.
  • Saved history (optional). When you tap Save, the calculation is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, together with the borrower name and note if you chose to type them. You can leave both empty.
  • Images and PDFs you share. When you choose to share a result, the app creates an image or a PDF on your phone and hands it to the app you pick in the Android share menu (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive). We do not see the file or where you send it.

This information is not uploaded to our servers. We do not run any servers for this app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, Google's ad software may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language), and
  • how ads are displayed and interacted with.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. How Google handles it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on your phone). Ads will still appear, but they will be less relevant to you.

4. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, precise location, messages or files. It has no sign-up or login. We do not use the app to track you.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad software, as described in section 3.

The app works for calculation and history without an internet connection.

6. How we use information

  • to calculate and display the result you ask for,
  • to keep your saved history on your phone, and
  • to display ads (by Google, as described above).

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your information. We do not receive your calculations or your saved history, so we have nothing of that kind to share. The only third party that handles data through the app is Google, for ads (section 3). Anything you send out using the share button goes only to the app and person you choose.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your history stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete a single entry or clear the whole history inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all data the app stored. Because we do not hold your data on a server, we cannot recover it if it is deleted.

9. Security

Saved data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, device encryption). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your phone locked and updated. Data that ad software sends over the internet is handled by Google.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. We do not store your personal data on our side, so your data is under your control in the app. For data handled by Google for ads, please use the controls in section 3 and Google's privacy tools. You can also contact us with any request.

12. Calculation disclaimer

The app is a calculation aid. Results depend on the dates, rate and method it uses (the rate is treated as a monthly rate, compounded yearly, with simple interest on the remaining months and days). Results are for information only and are not legal, financial or accounting advice. Please check important figures before relying on them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to nadafsahiman@gmail.com.
